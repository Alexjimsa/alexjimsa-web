# Hi! I'm Alex 👋


I am a geologist educated at the Complutense University of Madrid and currently completing a Master's Degree in Mineral and Energy Resources Exploration.

I am particularly interested in the application of Geographic Information Systems (GIS), spatial analysis, and digital tools to challenges related to geology, resource exploration, environmental studies, and land management.

In my projects, I combine geological knowledge with tools such as QGIS, Python, and modern web technologies to analyze, visualize, and communicate spatial information in a clear and meaningful way.

On this website, I share some of my projects, experiences, and interests as I continue building my professional profile at the intersection of geology, data, and technology.
